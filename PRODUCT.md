# Data Center Operation & Energy Inspection Web Application (DCIM Patrol)

## Overview
A mission-critical enterprise web application and Progressive Web App (PWA) designed for Data Center routine patrol, facility inspections, climate and energy monitoring, and asset defect reporting.

## Target Users
- **Data Center Operators & Field Technicians:** Perform routine facility walks, scan rack QR codes, inspect CRAC/PDU/UPS/Battery units, record measurements in areas with low or zero connectivity, and flag equipment defects.
- **Facility Engineers & Admins:** Oversee data center infrastructure health, approve operator registrations, track ASHRAE thermal compliance, review energy consumption trends (kW), manage rooms and equipment presets, and print QR codes.

## Brand Personality & Aesthetic Values
- **Disciplined & Architectural:** Clean lines, geometric typography, structured layouts, and deliberate whitespace reflecting high-reliability physical infrastructure.
- **Calm & Uncluttered:** Minimalist white-paper enterprise system that eliminates visual fatigue during long monitoring shifts.
- **Purposeful Signal:** Uses HP Electric Blue (#024ad8) strictly as the signal call-to-action, avoiding extraneous decorative colors or rainbow gradients.
- **High Legibility & Contrast:** Near-black ink (#1a1a1a) on pure white (#ffffff) canvas and crisp cloud/fog surfaces (#f7f7f7, #e8e8e8).

## Core Principles
1. **Utility First:** Fast data capture, single-tap navigation between inspection checkpoints, and zero superfluous steps.
2. **Resilience & Offline Autonomy:** Fully operational in offline shielded server rooms with seamless background reconciliation.
3. **Transparent Hierarchy:** Clear status signals with distinct semantic roles (normal, pending, warning, defect).
4. **Consistency:** Strict adherence to DESIGN.md design tokens, typography, and component specs.
